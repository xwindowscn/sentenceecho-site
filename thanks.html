付款完成 · 如何激活

Payment complete — how to activateKeep this page open until the app shows “已授权”.

① 打开 app：从「应用程序」里打开 SentenceEcho Voice / 逐句。 Open the app from your Applications folder.
② 看状态：授权窗口里如果已显示「已授权」，就完成了，什么都不用做。 If the licence window already shows “licensed”, you are done.
③ 还没生效：把收据邮件里的交易号（txn_ 开头）粘进「我已付款，刷新状态」，点刷新。 Paste the transaction ID from your receipt email (starts with txn_) and press refresh.
④ 找不到交易号：只填付款邮箱，点「恢复购买」，系统会把授权自动绑到这台电脑。 No transaction ID? Enter the purchase email and press “Restore purchase”.
换电脑：在新电脑上填同一个邮箱即可迁移，旧电脑自动失效。 Moving to a new Mac: enter the same email there; the old machine is revoked automatically.
